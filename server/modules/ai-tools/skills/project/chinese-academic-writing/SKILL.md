# Chinese academic writing

Use accurate, formal, restrained, clear, and logically connected Chinese academic expression. Keep terminology consistent, avoid colloquial wording, empty slogans, promotional claims, and unnecessary repetition.

Protect the source text's facts, data, technical terms, formulas, citations, and conclusions. Do not invent evidence or broaden claims.
